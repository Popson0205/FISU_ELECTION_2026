FISUEC eligibility criteria

FISUEC 2026 Publication 002 · 4/10/2026

1. General academic qualification

A candidate must hold, or be currently pursuing, at least one of: National Diploma (ND, preliminary included), National Certificate of Education (NCE), Higher National Diploma (HND), or B.Sc./B.A./LL.B./B.Tech. or an equivalent.

2. President

Must be an HND holder or undergraduate, or a B.Sc./B.A. graduate or undergraduate, or hold an equivalent qualification.

3. Treasurer and Auditor

Must be studying Accountancy, Accounting or a related course at a recognised institution, or have graduated in a related field and be a qualified accountant.

Offices available

    All candidates must meet the stipulated academic requirements to be considered eligible. Signed: Comr. Oyedeji Toyeeb Akolade (Chairman), Comr. Ojo Ifeoluwa (Vice Chairman), Comr. Marvelous Folorunsho (Secretary), Comr. Adeniran Wadud (PRO), Comr. Popoola Idris (Member).
